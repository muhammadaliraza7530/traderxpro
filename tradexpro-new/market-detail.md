# Market Detail Page

The Market Detail page is a responsive frontend preview for BTC/USDT-style market data. It does not submit real orders or connect to a market API.

## Routes

- `/market-detail?symbol=BTCUSDT`
- `/market-detail?pair=BTC/USDT&symbol=BTCUSDT`

The page includes selectable chart timeframes, a lightweight SVG candlestick chart, favorites persistence, and demo buy/sell actions.
